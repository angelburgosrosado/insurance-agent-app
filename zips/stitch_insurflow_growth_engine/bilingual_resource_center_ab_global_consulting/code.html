Common Questions / Preguntas Frecuentes

What is the difference between Term and Whole Life? expand_more
Term life insurance provides coverage for a specific period (e.g., 20 years), while Whole Life is permanent coverage that includes a cash value component. Term is typically more affordable, whereas Whole Life offers lifelong protection and a living benefit through equity growth.
¿Cómo funcionan las anualidades indexadas? expand_more
Las anualidades indexadas ofrecen un equilibrio entre seguridad y crecimiento. Su rendimiento está vinculado a un índice bursátil (como el S&P 500), pero cuentan con una garantía de que el capital principal no disminuirá incluso si el mercado cae.
Do I need a medical exam for life insurance? expand_more
Not necessarily. While traditional policies may require a medical exam, we offer many "no-medical-exam" options that utilize accelerated underwriting for faster approval based on your health history.
