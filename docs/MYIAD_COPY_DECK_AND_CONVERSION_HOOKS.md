## 4. Targeted Product Copy Decks

### 4.1 Product Line 1: Life Insurance & Wealth Preservation

**Sub-Categories:** Term Life with Living Benefits, Indexed Universal Life (IUL), Whole Life.

- **Headline:** `Permanent Protection Architecture & Tax-Exempt Accumulation`  
- **Sub-Headline:** `Engineered under Internal Revenue Code Section 7702 to deliver unconditional family security and market-insulated liquidity.`  

#### Feature & Benefit Copy Deck:

1. **Indexed Universal Life (IUL) with 0% Market Floor:**  
   * *Core Mechanism:* Premium dollars earn interest crediting linked to major market indices (such as the S&P 500) subject to a contractual annual floor of 0%. When markets drop 20%, your policy credits 0%, never negative.  
   * *Tax Structure:* Cash accumulation grows tax-deferred and can be accessed via non-taxable policy loans and withdrawals under IRC Section 7702 and 72(e), providing private supplemental retirement cash flow without IRS 10% early withdrawal penalties.  
   * *Target Audience:* Professionals, business owners, and estate planners seeking growth without equity downside risk.  

2. **Term Life with Accelerated Living Benefits:**  
   * *Core Mechanism:* High-coverage term protection (10, 20, or 30-year terms) equipped with comprehensive living benefit riders for qualifying Critical Illness (heart attack, stroke, invasive cancer), Chronic Illness (inability to perform 2 of 6 ADLs), or Terminal Illness (life expectancy under 12-24 months).  
   * *Benefit:* Accelerates up to 100% of the policy face amount during your lifetime to replace lost income, cover experimental medical treatments, or satisfy mortgage obligations without liquidating family savings.  

3. **Whole Life & Estate Liquidity:**  
   * *Core Mechanism:* Guaranteed level death benefits, guaranteed level annual premiums, and guaranteed annual cash-value growth schedules.  
   * *Benefit:* Supplies irrevocable estate liquidity to fund capital gains liabilities, transfer intergenerational wealth, or fund buy-sell agreements.

- **Product CTA:** `Compare Life & IUL Options`  
- **Required Verification Note:** Cash-value loans reduce available death benefits and cash value. Interest rates apply to policy loans. Indexed accounts credit interest based on index performance subject to caps, participation rates, and spreads set by the issuing insurer.
